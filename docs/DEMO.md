## Fechamento Claro | Open Agent Hackathon 2026

Fechamento Claro helps small business owners and bookkeepers investigate settlement differences. I am Bruno Fernandes, an Accounting graduate and solo builder. This is an edited walkthrough of the working local prototype. All records shown here are fictional.

## 1. Query and check the sources

The example loads orders, settlements and refunds. The local Zetaris platform registers fresh source snapshots, reads the original rows and joins their amounts through its official JDBC driver. Independent integer cent rules check every counted total. This close has three explained orders, two source reviews, one order not yet due, and one hundred twenty six reais and forty centavos in open due differences.

## 2. Inspect the original evidence

Order A one thousand two shows the distinction between a calculation and a cause. Two hundred reais, minus the stated six reais fee, gives an expected settlement of one hundred ninety four. The supplied settlement records one hundred eighty four. The remaining ten reais is unexplained. The app retains the exact cells and physical line numbers, and suggests checking the processor statement and fee contract.

## 3. Ask the local investigator

The question goes to Meterless Nightshift on this computer. A local Llama three point one, eight billion parameter model selects a search subject. Real Meterless H MEM retrieval supplies the available findings. The model then selects source inspection and completion. Only inspected evidence can appear in the answer. Rules supply all returned amounts, citations and next checks.

## 4. Inspect the execution trail

The expandable trail shows the actual tool calls and local model receipts. Invalid choices fail closed. The browser preview runs rules and evidence search; the full Zetaris and Nightshift workflow requires the local workspace. This prototype has no bank connection and moves no money.
